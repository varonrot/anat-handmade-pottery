LEARN WITH ANAT

More than a pottery class

At Anat Pottery, pottery is about slowing down, working with your hands and discovering what you can create. Classes are practical and supportive: you will not simply watch a demonstration — you will make your own pieces.

Learn by doing

Work directly with clay and receive guidance as you practise techniques yourself.

Small, supportive learning

Ask questions, get feedback and build confidence without feeling rushed.

From clay to finished piece

Explore forming, finishing, glazing and the journey from raw clay to a finished ceramic.

A welcoming studio

Come as a beginner, return as a regular, or use the studio experience to deepen your existing skills.

FAQ

Pottery classes in London — common questions

Do I need previous pottery experience?

No. Beginner classes are designed for people who have never worked with clay before.

Can beginners learn wheel throwing?

Yes. With step-by-step guidance, beginners can learn the foundations and start creating on the wheel.

Can I book a pottery class for two?

Yes. Our Pottery for 2 experience is designed for two people who want to create together.

Should I choose a class or a course?

Choose a class or workshop if you want to try pottery. Choose a course if you want structured learning and more time to develop your skills.
